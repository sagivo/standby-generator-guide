## Where Generac wins

**Availability and price.** Generac's scale means lower prices, stock on Amazon with Prime-speed freight, and an installer on every corner. If your Generac needs warranty work, someone nearby can do it.

**Fuel honesty.** Generac's kW ratings hold better across fuels — less derate anxiety when you're on natural gas.

## Where Kohler wins

**The enclosure.** Composite, corrosion-proof, impact-rated to -30°F. On the coast or in harsh weather, this is genuinely better than painted metal.

**PowerBoost.** Extra starting current for big motors without browning out the house — the AC kicks on and the lights don't flicker.

**Quietness.** The 20RESCL's 64 dB(A) exercise cycle is the quietest weekly self-test we've seen.

## The verdict

Buy Generac for the network and the price. Buy Kohler for the build quality and the coast. And whichever you choose, buy the size your [kW calculation](/tools/kw-size-calculator/) says — brand loyalty never kept the lights on.

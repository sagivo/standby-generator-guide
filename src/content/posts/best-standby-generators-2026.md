## How to choose

**Size first.** Everything else is secondary to getting the kW right. Add up your running watts plus your largest motor's starting surge (usually the AC), add 20–25% margin, and round up to the next standard size. Our [kW size calculator](/tools/kw-size-calculator/) does it in two minutes, and the [sizing guide](/posts/standby-generator-sizing-guide/) explains the method.

**Then fuel.** On propane you get the headline number; on natural gas several models derate by ~2 kW. A "20 kW" unit that's 18 kW on your fuel with a 19 kW load is a tripping generator.

**Then budget reality.** The unit is roughly half the project. Installation — transfer switch wiring, gas line, panel work, pad, permits — typically adds $4,000–$8,000. The [installation cost estimator](/tools/install-cost-estimator/) gives you the honest all-in number before you call an installer.

The Kohler 26RCAL is Kohler's flagship answer to the Guardian 26kW — and in several ways the more interesting machine. A corrosion-proof composite enclosure, PowerBoost motor-starting technology, and a 5-year/2,000-hour warranty make it the pick for buyers who want the best-engineered option, not just the most popular one.

## Who the 26RCAL is for

Buy it if you want maximum power with premium build quality — especially on propane, where it delivers the full 26 kW. Coastal homeowners should pay attention: the composite enclosure is genuinely corrosion-proof, unlike painted steel.

Note the fuel derate: on natural gas it's a 24 kW unit. That's still plenty, but size accordingly.

## PowerBoost and the enclosure

Kohler's PowerBoost technology provides extra starting current for big motors — central AC compressors, well pumps — without dimming the rest of the house. It's the kind of feature you only appreciate during the first outage, when the AC kicks on and the lights don't flicker.

## Transfer switch and monitoring

Bundled kits pair the generator with Kohler's 200A RXT automatic transfer switch with load shedding. OnCue Plus provides remote monitoring from anywhere — Kohler's answer to Mobile Link.

## Ownership notes

- **Warranty:** 5-year/2,000-hour limited — the hour cap matters if you expect long outages; Generac's is time-only.
- **Dealer network:** Smaller than Generac's. Confirm a certified installer near you before buying.
- **Price:** Usually a premium over the equivalent Guardian. You're paying for the enclosure and engineering.

The Champion 14kW aXis is the value disruptor of home standby: the quietest unit here at 62 dB(A), a 10-year warranty nobody else matches, and the lowest entry price — with smart load management that stretches 14 kW further than the number suggests.

## Who the Champion 14kW is for

Buy it if you're budget-conscious, noise-sensitive, or in a cold climate. It's ideal for homes up to ~1,800 sq ft where you're happy to back up essentials plus one AC rather than the entire panel at once.

Skip it if you want true run-everything backup for a large home — that's a 22 kW+ job.

## aXis load management

The aXis platform is the secret sauce: the transfer switch communicates with load-management modules over your home's existing wiring (no extra control wires), assigning each big appliance a priority. Near capacity, lower-priority loads shed automatically. It's how 14 kW covers a home that looks like it needs 18.

## Cold weather and noise

The 24-volt starting system (double the cranking power of typical 12V setups) starts at -22°F with no cold-weather kit — a genuine advantage in the snow belt. And 62 dB(A) is conversation-quiet; this is the unit your neighbors will complain about least.

## Ownership notes

- **Warranty:** 10-year limited on the generator — the longest standard warranty in home standby — plus free lifetime tech support.
- **Fuel:** 14 kW on propane, 12.5 kW on natural gas.
- **ATS options:** 100A, 150A, or 200A aXis switches — match it to your panel.

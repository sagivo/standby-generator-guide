The Generac Guardian 26kW (model 7291) is the flagship of the best-selling home standby generator line in the US — and our top overall pick. With 26 kW of standby power, a 200-amp transfer switch in the box, and Wi-Fi monitoring included, it's the closest thing to a default answer for large-home backup.

## Who the Guardian 26kW is for (and who should skip it)

Buy it if your home is 2,500+ sq ft with central air and you want everything running through an outage: multiple AC zones, the well pump, the electric range, even an EV charger. This is true whole-house backup with margin to spare.

Skip it if your home is under ~2,000 sq ft or your load calculation lands under 18 kW. The 26kW costs more, weighs 518 lb (freight delivery), and burns more fuel than a right-sized unit. The [22kW Guardian](/generators/generac-guardian-22kw/) or [18kW Guardian](/generators/generac-guardian-18kw/) covers most suburbs for less.

## Power and fuel

The purpose-built G-Force 1000 Series V-twin (999cc) is designed specifically for generator duty — not a repurposed lawn-mower engine — which is a big part of why Generac leads on reliability reputation. It runs on natural gas or liquid propane, starts automatically within seconds of an outage, and runs as long as fuel holds out.

## Transfer switch and monitoring

The 7291 kit includes a 200A service-entrance-rated automatic transfer switch, so there's no separate ATS purchase to forget. Wi-Fi connectivity comes standard now, with Generac's Mobile Link letting you check status, exercise history, and maintenance reminders from your phone.

## Noise and installation

Expect around 67 dB(A) — you'll hear it, but it won't dominate the backyard. Budget for professional installation: at this size, the electrical work, gas plumbing, pad, and permits typically add $4,000–$8,000. Our [installation cost estimator](/tools/install-cost-estimator/) breaks it down.

## Ownership notes

- **Warranty:** 5-year limited, backed by 24/7/365 support and 6,000+ dealers — the densest service network in the business.
- **Maintenance:** Annual oil/filter/plug service; the weekly exercise cycle is automatic.
- **Battery:** Not included — a crank battery and preferably a [battery maintainer](/accessories/) are essential; dead batteries are the #1 no-start cause.

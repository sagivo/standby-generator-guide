The Generac Guardian 18kW is the right-size Guardian for most suburbs: enough for a 1,500–2,500 sq ft home's essentials plus central air, with the 200A transfer switch and Wi-Fi monitoring included, at a clear discount to the 22kW+ models.

## Who the Guardian 18kW is for

Buy it if your load calculation lands at or under 18 kW with margin — the classic case is a 3-bedroom suburban home with one AC unit, gas heat or a heat pump, and standard appliances. If the math is close, the [22kW](/generators/generac-guardian-22kw/) is the safer step up.

## What you give up vs. the 22kW

About 4 kW and some headroom for simultaneous big loads (AC + electric dryer + oven at once). What you keep: the same G-Force engine family, the same 5-year warranty, the same dealer network, the same included ATS and monitoring.

## Sizing discipline

The most common 18kW regret is aspirational sizing — buying it for a home that really needs 22 kW because the price was tempting. Run the [kW size calculator](/tools/kw-size-calculator/) honestly, including the AC's starting surge. A tripping generator is worse than no generator.

## Ownership notes

- **Warranty:** 5-year limited, 24/7/365 support.
- **Installation:** Same $4,000–$8,000 installed-project math as its siblings — the unit savings are real, the install savings are modest.
- **Battery:** Not included; add a crank battery and maintainer.

The Kohler 20RESCL is the refined mid-size pick: 20 kW of standby power (on propane), the quietest weekly exercise cycle we've seen at 64 dB(A), and a corrosion-proof enclosure — at a mid-range price. It's the generator for people who notice details.

## Who the 20RESCL is for

Buy it if your home is 1,800–2,200 sq ft and you want Kohler quality without flagship pricing. The 64 dB(A) exercise cycle is the standout feature — your neighbors will barely register the weekly self-test, which matters in dense suburbs with noise ordinances.

## The natural-gas derate

On propane: 20 kW. On natural gas: 18 kW. If you're on NG, do your load calculation against 18 kW. This is the single most important spec on this page — an 18 kW budget with a 19 kW load is a tripping generator.

## Engine and enclosure

The Command PRO CH1000 is a 999cc, 32-hp V-twin with hydraulic lifters (no valve adjustments, no break-in period) and full-pressure lubrication with an oil cooler for long unattended runs. The composite enclosure is corrosion-proof and impact-tested to -30°F — equally at home on the coast and in the snow belt.

## Transfer switch and monitoring

The 200SELS kit includes Kohler's 200A RXT service-entrance ATS with load shedding, and OnCue Plus gives you remote monitoring from your phone.

## Ownership notes

- **Warranty:** 5-year/2,000-hour limited.
- **Maintenance:** Oil and filter every 150 hours or annually; air filter and plugs every 300 hours or annually.
- **Weight:** 500 lb — freight delivery, plan the pad location first.
